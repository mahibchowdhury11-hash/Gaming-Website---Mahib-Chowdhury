Hollow Knight

A singleplayer platformer game where you can fight many different enemies with supernatural powers and the goal is to confront the infection known as the Radiance.

Gameplay

This is a platformer/Metroidvania game where good movement is really useful. There are many enemies to fight and tons of abilities. You start off in as a bug and your goal is to get to end where you fight the final boss named the Radiance. This is a really nice looking boss fight that I have seen before. There is a sequel of this game where you play as one of the bosses from an early bossfight. Some similar games to Hollow knight are, Blasphemous(1 and 2), Ender Lilies: Quietus of the Knights, Nine Sols and many more amazing games can be found that are pretty similar to Hollow Knight.
