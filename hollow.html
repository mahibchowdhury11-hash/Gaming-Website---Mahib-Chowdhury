Hollow Knight

A singleplayer platformer game where you can fight many different enemies with supernatural powers and the goal is to confront the infection known as the Radiance.

Gameplay

This is a platformer/Metroidvania game where good movement is really useful. There are many enemies to fight and tons of abilities. You start off as a bug and your goal is to confront the final boss who is spreading illness to everyone. All of the boss fights are really cool and they are pretty different from eachother which forces the players to find new ways to overcome each challenge. There is a sequel of the game called, Silksong where you play as the character Hornet. So in conclusion I would definitly recommend this game.
