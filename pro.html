Pros and Cons

Hollow Knight

Pros

  • Very nice graphics
  • Huge and Amazing map
  • 30 - 60 hours of playtime to beat
  • Very good combat and abilities well made
  • Changable stats at all times(charms)
  • Can make up for deaths
  • Good value, price and timewise
  • Amazing Soundtracks

Cons

  • Costs money
  • Requires skill(Not really a bad thing)
  • Not much guidance(Use youtube or ask a friend)


In conclusion this game has many more pros and can easily be seen as a great game.

Roblox

Pros

  • Free
  • Lots of creativity and options on what to play
  • Lots of customization with appearance
  • Possible way to make money
  • Can make friends(maybe)

Cons

  • Chat requires ID
  • Lots of AI slop games(Same game ideas almost no creativity)
  • Most games are mean't to be addicting
  • Lots of pay to win games
  • Bad moderation
  • Multiplayer

Roblox has a mixed amount of Pros and Cons but I still think it is a good game, cause the bad things aren't enough to overshadow the good things. For example you can use AI to be creative but that isn't happening. But if that is done I think the games on roblox will increase in overall quality. So in conclusion Roblox is a good game even if it isn't near its peak at the moment.

Brawlhalla

Pros

  • Free
  • Multiplayer
  • Variety of Gamemodes
  • Unique combat system
  • 70+ characters
  • Has many crossovers with other games/series

Cons

  • Unbalanced charaters
  • Lots of spammming at low ranks
  • Lots of passive play styles at high ranks
  • Good wifi required

In conclusion we can say that Brawlhalla is a good game. Most of its problems come from the players. There is lots of passive and spamming in global matches. So this is why I mainly recommended playing this game only with your friends. But I still recommend this game.

Celeste

Pros

  • Tons of Mods
  • Low system requirement
  • Lots of techs to master after completing the game
  • Amazing soundtracks
  • Assitance offered

Cons

  • High Skill required(Not really a bad thing)

We can tell that Celeste is a really good game, which is why I recommend it. I couldn't think of any more cons because the game is well built. Maybe there are more issues with Mods, but from my Point of View it looks like an amazing game.

Streets of Rogue

Pros

  • Unique Gameplay
  • Many characters to choose from
  • Multiplayer
  • Mutators(Built in Modifiers you can choose from)

Cons

  • Game might get repetitive

Streets of Rogue is a really good game but overtime the game might start to get repetitive or boring because there are a few alternating tasks. But this game has a decent amount of content and I still really like the game, also if you find friends to play this game with it makes it even better.
