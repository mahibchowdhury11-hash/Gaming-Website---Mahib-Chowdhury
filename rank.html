RANKINGS

Experience

(Lifetime)

  1. Roblox(1500+ hours). I have been playing this game for most of my life with and without my friends. I started playing Brawlhalla later and the other games even more later after I got a better device.
  2. Brawlhalla(400 hours). I would play this game with my friend because he had a Nintendo Switch and my mom would babysit him so he would visit my home frequently. I started playing this game pretty quickly after getting my own laptop. I don't play it as much anymore but it is still a really good game.
  3. Street of Rogue(60+ hours). This is a really good game, I started playing the game kind of recently so about a few months ago. I play it kind of often still but not as much since school started even tho the game is amazing.
  4. Celeste(5 hours). I downloaded this game pretty recently but I haven't gotten too much time to play the game but I will definitly do that more.
  5. Hollow Knight(5 hours). I have played this game before on other peoples devices but I don't own a copy of Hollow Knight myself so I have barely any time on the game. I still think the game is really amazing based on seeing people do boss fights. I mainly have time on this game from my friends Nintendo Switch.

Recommendation

  1. Street of Rogue. This game is really unique and I haven't really found other games similar to Street of Rogue. Since this game has characters with abilities that are pretty unique. Same goes for the maps, you don't know what quest and layout your going to get. And on every third floor there is a special thing always going on. So maybe a riot, bombs falling, poison goo, and more. So I really recommend this game because of how fun and unique this game is.
  2. Celeste. This game is fun to play and has cool movements, the stuff on the game look really cool and there are many techs(unofficial skills) to become better and better at the game. I have looked at some speed runners and they make the game look even cooler.
  3. Hollow Knight. The game has a beautiful design and the attacks are really cool, same with the enemies. This game is definitly worth getting.
  4. Roblox. Despite the game being free and really accesable, the game isn't in a very good state at the moment, since most of the games being made right now are mainly just brainrot games. Also roblox added age verification which I did, but still really takes away from the experience since communication with random people is fun and also pretty necessary in most of the games.
  5. Brawlhalla. I wouldn't recommend this game way too much since it takes lots of time too become good at, and there is nothing really to achieve in the end other then becoming amazing and attending tournaments, then making money. I still recommend this game because it is fun to play with friends but if your a busy person I probably wouldn't recommend playing this game competitively.
