Experience

(Lifetime)

  1. Roblox (1500+ hours). I have been playing this game for most of my life with and without my friends. I started playing Brawlhalla later and the other games even more later after I got a better device.

  2. Brawlhalla (400 hours). I would play this game with my friend because he had a Nintendo Switch and my mom would babysit him so he would visit my home frequently. I started playing this game pretty quickly after getting my own laptop. I don't play it as much anymore, but it is still a really good game.

  3. Streets of Rogue (60+ hours). This is a really good game. I started playing it kind of recently, about a few months ago. I play it kind of often still, but not as much since school started, even though the game is amazing.

  4. Celeste (5 hours). I downloaded this game pretty recently, but I haven't gotten too much time to play it, I will definitely do that more.

  5. Hollow Knight (5 hours). I have played this game before on other people's devices, but I don't own a copy of Hollow Knight myself, so I have barely any time on the game. I still think the game is really amazing based on seeing people do boss fights. I mainly have time on this game from my friend's Nintendo Switch.

Recommendation

  1. Streets of Rogue. This game is really unique and I haven't really found other games similar to it. The characters have abilities that are pretty unique, and the same goes for the maps—you never know what quest and layout you're going to get. On every third floor, there is a special thing always going on (like a riot, bombs falling, poison goo, and more). So I really recommend this game because of how fun and unique it is.

  2. Celeste. This game is fun to play and has cool movement. The stuff in the game looks really cool and there are many techs (unofficial skills) to become better and better at the game. I have looked at some speedrunners and they make the game look even cooler.

  3. Hollow Knight. The game has a beautiful design and the attacks are really cool, same with the enemies. This game is definitely worth getting.

  4. Roblox. Despite the game being free and really accessible, the game isn't in a very good state at the moment, since most of the games being made right now are mainly just brainrot games. Also, Roblox added age verification which I did, but it still really takes away from the experience since communication with random people is fun and also pretty necessary in most of the games.

  5. Brawlhalla. I wouldn't recommend this game too much since it takes a lot of time to become good at it, and there is nothing really to achieve in the end other than becoming amazing, attending tournaments, and making money. I still recommend this game because it is fun to play with friends, but if you're a busy person. I probably wouldn't recommend playing it competitively.
