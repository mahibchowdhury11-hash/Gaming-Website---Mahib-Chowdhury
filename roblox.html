Roblox

A multiplayer 3d game where there are millions of games that you can choose from made the users of Roblox.

Gameplay

Roblox is a really popular game with over 10 billion players, but a chunk of that is probably just bots but the estimated amount might be atleast 1 or 2 billion players. The game has really changed over time but it is still pretty good. This is a really good game to play with friends or alone. But the main part about this game is that it has millions of games to choose between from tons of catagories. I would recommend this game to anyone who wants a unique experience every time they open up a brand new game.




Price: Free!
