Gameplay

This game is really unique, with 32 characters(6 of them cost money). The goal in this game is to overthrow the mayor. You spawn in the first level named slums where there are three floors in that level. The floor layouts are randomized so you never know exactly what to expect unless you put in a specific seed. You go around and do the few tasks(2 or 3) that you are assigned for the floor. I won't say much more because I don't want to spoil the entire game but I would really recommend this game. It is pretty niche and I haven't met anyone in real life who plays this game.

Price: $25.99 CAD or $18.60 USD
